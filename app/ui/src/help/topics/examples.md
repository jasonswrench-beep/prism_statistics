# Open Example Data

Opens one of the datasets built into PrismStatistics, for practice and for teaching. Variable labels, value labels, measures and missing-value codes are already set, so the dialogs and output read as they would with well-prepared data.

## How to open it
Choose **File › Open Example Data…**, or **Example data** on the Home screen. Each dataset lists what it holds and analyses to try. Choose **Open** beside one. If the open project has unsaved changes, you are asked first.

## The datasets
- **Study methods**: 120 students taught by lecture or active learning. Pretest, posttest and exam scores, study hours, absences, satisfaction (with −99 for no answer, set as missing) and a five-item test-anxiety scale with one reverse-worded item. Use it for t tests, ANOVA, correlation, regression, logistic regression, crosstabs and reliability.
- **Sleep and reaction time**: 40 volunteers measured on four days of short sleep, half taking caffeine. Use it for repeated-measures ANOVA, Friedman and mixed designs.
- **Clinic follow-up**: 80 patients on two treatments, with weeks until relapse, including censored cases. Use it for Kaplan-Meier and Cox regression.

## Good to know
- The data are simulated. They were invented for teaching and describe no real people.
- An example opens as new, unsaved data. Save it as a project to keep your work.
- The **Worked examples** in Help walk through analyses of the Study methods data step by step: [comparing two groups](topic:two-groups), [before and after](topic:before-after) and [a relationship and a prediction](topic:relationship).
